# Copy audit, 8 October 2026

This audit covers the words a seller reads in MyShopEdge. It covers every screen in `web/src`, every message the service can return, the plan cards, the CSV exports and the public landing page at `site/index.html`. The pages Stack Auth hosts for sign-in are Stack's own, so they are outside it.

No copy was changed. Each finding quotes the current text with its file and line, and suggests a replacement for the owner to approve.

## The standard applied

The audit applies two layers. Where they conflict, the first wins, and the conflict is recorded.

1. **The house style.** Every sentence carries a subject and a verb. No em dashes and no en dashes are used. No clipped marketing phrases are used. Modest things are described honestly. Nobody is praised by diminishing anybody else. Terms follow A8, with A29.7 where the owner ruled later.
2. **The bar set by Stripe, Netflix and similar products.** Text is clear on first reading and shows no internal codes. Every button says what will happen. Every error says what happened, whether anything was lost or charged, and what to do next. Every screen has its loading, empty, error and success states. The same thing has the same name everywhere. Every promise is backed by code that exists. Text is concise and accessible.

## What matters most

Most of the blocking findings are not wording faults. They are promises the product does not keep, or figures shown in a way that misleads. Rewording alone does not fix those. Each needs a decision: build the thing, or withdraw the promise.

### 3. Errors and states

- **Validation errors crash forms.** Alert settings and Export show the service's `detail` as the error message, and for a validation error that `detail` is an array. A blank threshold or blank export dates would therefore break the form. The array was confirmed by running the service; the crash has not been seen in a browser.
- **Error titles and some details are internal.** Every error's `title` is its code, such as `token_unverifiable`. Some details name environment variables, or say "not configured on this deployment".
- **One error is shown wherever access is refused.** Every 403 other than `account_closing` is shown as "That shop is not on your account.", including errors about the account.
- **Some screens have no recovery.** No route has a loading page or an error page, and no form in the core screens confirms success.
- **One failure has many wordings.** A currency mismatch is worded five ways, a period in the wrong order three ways, and a missing cost four ways.

### 4. Legal and trust

- **No privacy notice or terms are linked.** Neither Start nor the landing page links them, and real accounts already exist on production. `start/page.jsx:12-15` says this must be closed before a real seller is invited.
- **The landing page gives no company number.** It also does not label the address as the registered office.
- **The delete screen contradicts itself.** It says "Signing in: Stops now", and on the same screen says the seller can sign in for thirty days.

## Decisions only the owner can make

1. **Withdraw or build.** For each promise in section 1, decide whether it is built or removed. Removing it is a copy change. Building it is code. Withdrawing "Scheduled exports" and "Priority support" also changes what each plan offers over the one below it.
2. **"Paid out" or "Settled to your bank."** A8.4 withdrew "Shop Money" and "Paid out". A29.7, ruled later, uses both. One of the two documents needs amending.
3. **Where to cancel.** A seller can stop renewal only by deleting the account. The billing copy cannot be made true without either a cancel action in Settings or new wording that names deletion.
4. **Scotland.** Either load the Scottish bands, or state on the tax screen that the estimate assumes an England, Wales or Northern Ireland taxpayer.
5. **Legal pages.** The privacy notice and terms are the owner's to write or commission. A company number on the site is to be checked with an adviser.

## Area A. Onboarding, account, billing and the shell


Audited 8 October 2026, read only. Every string below is quoted from the file at the line given. Paths are relative to `/workspace/My-ShopEdge/web/src` unless they start with `service/`. Where a promise was checked against the code, the search is named in the Problem column.

### Cross-cutting patterns

1. **Three promises about billing have nothing behind them.** The trial screens promise a reminder email three days before the first charge, and they say a seller can cancel the trial and change the card in Settings. No code sends email (`service/app/account_deletion.py:47-48` says "nothing in the service sends email"). Settings has no cancel button and no change-card button. The only way to stop the plan renewing is Delete my account. The Settings profile page says this itself at `app/shops/[shopId]/settings/profile/page.jsx:7-8`.
2. **The first read of a shop is described as immediate, and it is not.** The callback says "We are reading your orders, returns and payouts now", and S2 says "This can take a few minutes". Nothing in `service/app/connections.py` starts a sync. A shop is first read by `run_due()` from the daily cron at 05:47 UTC, or by a TikTok webhook event. A seller who connects in the morning can wait almost a day.
3. **The plan cards sell things that do not exist.** "Scheduled exports" and "Priority support" appear only in `service/app/plans.py` and nowhere else in the service or the front end. The "Most chosen" flag is a hard-coded `highlight=True` and rests on no data.
4. **Some links loop back to where the seller started.** For a seller with one shop, "Your shops" leads to `/shops`, which redirects straight back into the shop (`app/shops/(list)/page.jsx:26-36`). For a seller whose plan has ended or whose payment failed, "Choose a plan" and "Try the card again" both lead to `/billing`. That page then says "There is nothing to choose here now."
5. **Terminology drifts from A8.** The copy uses "what is left after TikTok", "the VAT line", "Packing" and "Postage", and A8 8.4 withdrew or replaced all of them. "Payouts", "payments and fees" and "statements" all name the same thing on different screens. A8 8.4 maps "Paid out" to "Settled to your bank", while A29 (24 September) uses "Paid out". **The two rulings conflict, and the owner needs to rule on it before the label can be fixed.** The brief's list of jargon includes "ledger", but A8 8.2 requires the correct accounting term. Layer 1 wins, so "Ledger" in the export kinds is not flagged.
6. **Many headings and helper lines are fragments.** Examples are "Read-only.", "Optional.", "Not tax advice." and "One moment". Headings also differ in whether they end with a full stop, sometimes on the same screen family. For example, "Your account is closing" has no full stop and "MyShopEdge could not be reached." has one.
7. **The service's raw `detail` text is shown to sellers as the fallback error** (PaymentForm, ConnectTikTok, the callback and every SetupForms action). One such detail is "File storage is not configured on this deployment." (`service/app/storage.py:58`). It is internal wording, and it gives no next step.
8. **Six screens render a `<main>` inside the layout's `<main id="main">`.** These are signed-out, closing, email-in-use, billing, confirmed and payment-failed. Assistive technology then finds two main landmarks.
9. **The sign-in methods are uncertain.** Start says "Google, GitHub or Microsoft". CLAUDE.md (7 October) says "Google only". The Stack project's configuration cannot be read from this session.

## Area B. Core shop screens


Scope: every file under `web/src/app/shops/[shopId]/` for Today, Stock (and `stock/[skuId]`), Returns, Products (and `[productId]`, `[productId]/transactions`), Money (`page.jsx` only), Payouts (and `[settlementId]`), Tax, Notifications, Discrepancies, Records, Glossary and `layout.jsx`, plus `components/Figure.jsx`, `LineLabel.jsx`, `AdjustForm.jsx`, `CostForm.jsx`, `InvoiceForm.jsx`, `NotificationActions.jsx`, `ResolveActions.jsx`, `ReturnCheckForm.jsx` and `lib/terms.js`. Strings that the service writes and these screens print verbatim are included, with the service file and line, because the seller reads them on these screens.

Method: every file was read in full. Promises were checked by searching `service/`. Nothing was executed. Findings that rest on reading service logic rather than on a run are marked **(read, not run)**, as CLAUDE.md rule 7 asks.

Paths below are relative to `web/src/app/shops/[shopId]/` unless they start with `components/`, `lib/` or `service/`.

### Cross-cutting patterns

1. **A cost entered today does not cost anything sold before today (read, not run).** `CostForm` sends no `effective_from` (`components/CostForm.jsx:30-34`), and `service/app/costs.py:111` defaults it to `business_today()`. The cost file path does the same (`service/app/cost_uploads.py:446,459`). No screen in the web app sends an `effective_from` (searched `web/src` for `effective_from`). Since A31.4 each unit is costed at the cost in force on its sale date (`service/app/products.py:218`), so a seller who adds a cost for a product that already sold still sees "One variant has no cost price yet." for that period, and an unsellable return check keeps failing with "Add its cost, then check the item again." Every "Add cost" call to action in this area therefore promises a result it cannot deliver for past sales, and no copy says the cost applies from today. This is the most serious finding, because it breaks trust in the first thing a new seller does.
2. **The same money carries different names on different screens.** Payout appears as "Paid out" (Today, Payouts), "Payout" (Money) and, under A8.4, should be "Settled to your bank". The reserve appears as "Held back by TikTok" (Payouts) and "Reserve withheld" (Money). Return postage appears as "Return postage deducted", "Return postage you paid" and "Return shipping you paid". An unrecognised fee has four names. The glossary table at the end lists them.
3. **A8 and A29.7 conflict, and layer 1 must be settled by the owner.** A8.4 withdrew "Shop Money" (now "Settlement") and "Paid out" (now "Settled to your bank"). A29.7, a later ruling in the owner's own words, uses "Shop Money" and "Paid out". Both are layer 1. This audit follows A29.7 as the later ruling and recommends that A8.4 be amended so the two agree.
4. **Signs are shown as the ledger stores them.** The payout and the reserve are stored negative (`service/app/settlements.py:14-17`, `service/app/tiktok_sync.py:450`). Money shows "Payout" as a red negative and Payouts shows "Held back by TikTok" as a negative, while Today shows the same payout as positive "Paid out". The service docstrings say the presentation is the screen's job, and the screens have not done it.
5. **Internal codes reach the seller through fallbacks.** Patterns such as `?? n.type`, `?? a.status`, `?? e.entry_type`, `e.source`, `r.rule_key`, TikTok's raw fee field names, the refresh failure code and OAuth scope names all print raw identifiers.
6. **The period is not stated where a figure depends on it.** Products, Product detail and Product transactions never say which dates they cover. Product transactions in fact covers all time while Product detail covers this month, so "every record behind these figures" does not add up to those figures. Today's Shop Money is all time and does not say so.
7. **Promises of future work have nothing behind them.** "will appear here shortly", "We are adding this year's HMRC threshold" and "we are identifying it" each describe work that no code performs.
8. **States are thin.** No `loading.jsx` or `error.jsx` exists anywhere under `web/src/app` (searched), so no screen has a loading state and an unexpected exception falls to the framework's default page. Money waits on a live TikTok call before it renders. No form in this area confirms success; each clears itself and refreshes. Form errors print the service's `detail` and fall back to "was not saved" with no next step.
9. **House style breaks in small, repeated ways.** Fragments without a verb appear in sub-lines and footnotes ("Not financial advice.", "Counted {date}.", "Product cost: not provided.", "Nothing new.", glossary definitions). The unknown-figure glyph in `Figure.jsx` is an em dash.

## Area C. Settings, data, account control and shared errors


Audited 8 October 2026, read-only. Paths are relative to `/workspace/My-ShopEdge/web/src` unless they start with `service/`, `schema/` or `api/`, which are relative to `/workspace/My-ShopEdge`.

Files covered: `app/shops/[shopId]/settings/page.jsx`, `settings/profile/page.jsx`, `settings/alerts/page.jsx`, `settings/data/page.jsx`, `settings/delete/page.jsx`, `settings/disconnect/page.jsx`, `app/shops/[shopId]/connection-problem/page.jsx`, `app/shops/[shopId]/other-sales/page.jsx`, `app/shops/[shopId]/money/export/page.jsx`, `components/SettingsForms.jsx`, `components/AccountDeletion.jsx`, `components/ApiProblem.jsx`, `lib/api.js`, `lib/money-input.js`, `lib/stack.js`. The data and export pages render `DataDownload`, `ExportForm` and `JobStatus` from `components/SetupForms.jsx` (lines 19 to 21 and 348 to 508), so those lines were audited too, because the seller reads them on these screens. `lib/api.js`, `lib/money-input.js` and `lib/stack.js` produce no seller-visible string; `lib/api.js` matters only because every form passes the service's `detail` straight to the screen (see pattern 1).

### How facts were obtained

- **The 422 shape was run, not read.** With the auth dependencies overridden in a `TestClient`, `PUT /v1/shops/{id}/alert-settings` with `low_stock_days: 0` answered `422 {"detail":[{"type":"greater_than_equal",...},...]}`, and `POST /v1/shops/{id}/exports` with empty dates answered `422 {"detail":[{"type":"date_from_datetime_parsing",...}]}`. `DELETE /v1/me` with `{}` answered the same array shape. `main.py:87-88` registers handlers for `HTTPException` and `Exception` only, so FastAPI's own handler for request validation stays in charge and `detail` is a list of objects, not a string.
- That React refuses to render a plain object as a child is React's documented behaviour. **The crash on these three forms has not been driven in a browser.**
- Everything else below was read from the file named in the row.

### Cross-cutting patterns

1. **Every client form shows the service's `detail` verbatim** (`SettingsForms.jsx:59, 112, 174`, `AccountDeletion.jsx:37, 89`, `SetupForms.jsx:20`). Three things go wrong. (a) A request validation failure gives `detail` as an array, which the forms put into state and render, so the form would throw instead of showing an error. Alert settings reaches this with an empty box, a 0 or any letters, and Export reaches it with "Choose dates" left blank. (b) Developer text reaches sellers: `service/app/auth.py:147-148` says "Authentication is not configured. Set NEON_AUTH_JWKS_URL to the jwks_url from the project's Neon Auth configuration." and `service/app/storage.py:58` says "File storage is not configured on this deployment." (c) A 401 in a form shows "Sign in to continue." with no sign-in link, and a 500 shows "Something went wrong at our end." with no word on whether anything was saved.
2. **`apiProblem` treats every 403 except `account_closing` as a foreign shop** (`ApiProblem.jsx:55-61`). The service also sends 403 for `account_suspended` (`auth.py:308`), `email_unverified` (`auth.py:333`) and `email_required` (`auth.py:381`). On Profile and plan, which reads `/me` and has no shop in it, each would read "That shop is not on your account."
3. **The generic fallback talks about numbers on pages that show none.** "We show nothing rather than risk showing a wrong number." (`ApiProblem.jsx:75`) appears on Disconnect, Delete, Download my data, Export, Alert settings and Profile, because they all call `apiProblem`. A 503 `identity_syncing` ("We are still setting up your account. Try again in a moment.", `auth.py:371`) loses its own, better wording to this line.
4. **Three promises have nothing behind them.** (a) "You can leave this screen and come back" (`SetupForms.jsx:368`): no screen lists past exports or data downloads. The contract has no list operation for either (`api/openapi.yaml` has `createExport`, `getExport`, `requestAccountExport`, `getAccountExport` only), and the job lives in component state, so leaving loses it. A second data request within five minutes is refused 409 `export_in_progress` (`service/app/exports.py:447`). (b) "Link works until {date}" (`SetupForms.jsx:392`) shows the seven-day `expires_at`, but the link the button holds is signed for fifteen minutes (`service/app/storage.py:12`, `SIGNED_URL_LIFE = timedelta(minutes=15)`). (c) "Coming back window (days)" (`SettingsForms.jsx:75`) saves `coming_back_days`, and no code in `service/app` other than `alert_settings.py` reads that column. The `coming_back` stock state is set by units in transit (`service/app/stock.py:184`).
5. **The deletion copy says more than the system does.** "Signing in: Stops now" sits beside "For thirty days you can sign in and keep your account" on the same screen. The ledger is said to be kept "for the period financial records must be kept", but A30.1 leaves that period open and `service/app/account_deletion.py:35-36` says nothing deletes it. "Erased after 30 days" depends on the Render cron job `My-ShopEdge-erase`, which CLAUDE.md records as failing on every run until the owner sets `DATABASE_URL` and the five R2 variables on it. That state could not be read from this session. The job also runs at 03:17 UTC, so an account becomes due during one day and is erased on the next run, not "On {day}".
6. **Disconnecting and deleting leave out what happens at TikTok and at Stripe.** Neither the disconnect handler nor `deleteMe` calls TikTok's DeauthorizeShop (`connections.py:513-517`, `account_deletion.py:49-50`), so MyShopEdge stays on the seller's list of authorised apps in TikTok. Disconnect does not touch billing (`connections.py:519-539` has no billing call), so the plan keeps renewing, and the disconnect screens do not say so.
7. **House style: page subtitles are fragments.** Five header lines have no main verb (settings, profile, data, export, other sales). Status lines "Saved.", "Building your file." and "Nothing entered yet." are also fragments. No em or en dashes were found in any file in the area (searched for the characters and for `&mdash;` and `&ndash;`).
8. **A8 terminology.** "Coming back" is withdrawn by A8.4 in favour of "Returns in transit" and still appears twice. "VAT monitor" stands where A8.4 requires HMRC's term, "VAT registration threshold". "Stored TikTok tokens" is internal jargon.
9. **Naming drifts across one flow.** The nav says "Your data" (`lib/nav.js:59`), the page title and the delete screen's link say "Download my data", and the button says "Prepare my data". The nav says "Alerts" (`lib/nav.js:58`), while the page says "Alert settings" and the settings hub says "Low stock alert". The service's voice is "We", while the front end mostly says "MyShopEdge".

## Area D. Service messages, plan text and the landing page


Audited 8 October 2026, read only. Scope: every human-readable string the Python service in `service/app/` can send to a seller, and `site/index.html`. Every `Problem(...)` and `problem_response(...)` call was extracted with an AST script (112 call sites in 23 files, more than the 87 estimated, because one call is often reused per branch). Each call is covered below, either as a finding or in the "meets the standard" list at the end of the main table.

Two things were run rather than read:

- `TestClient` against `app.main:app` with a bad token returned `{"type":".../token_unverifiable","title":"token_unverifiable","status":401,"detail":"Sign in again.","code":"token_unverifiable"}`. The `title` is the machine code.
- `TestClient` with `require_account` overridden, sending `PATCH /v1/notifications/not-a-uuid` with `{"status":"bogus"}`, returned `422 application/json` with `{"detail":[{"type":"uuid_parsing",...,"msg":"Input should be a valid UUID, invalid character: found `n` at 1",...}]}`. That is FastAPI's default shape, not a problem document.

### Cross-cutting patterns

1. **The `title` of every problem is the machine code.** `problems.py:27` sets `"title": code`, so a seller who sees the title reads `validation_failed` or `token_unverifiable`. The module's own docstring (`problems.py:3-5`) says `title` and `detail` "are for humans". Severity: Major.
2. **Request validation errors bypass the problem format.** `main.py:87-88` registers handlers for `HTTPException` and `Exception` only. FastAPI's own `RequestValidationError` handler still answers, with a JSON array of Pydantic messages such as "Input should be 'read' or 'done'". The web forms show `r.data?.detail` (`web/src/components/CostForm.jsx:37`, `AdjustForm.jsx:42`, `ConnectTikTok.jsx:42`), which is an array in this case, so the seller sees either Pydantic's English or nothing usable. Severity: Major.
3. **Configuration faults leak into seller text.** Nine call sites tell a seller that something "is not configured" or "is not available on this deployment", and three name environment variables (`auth.py:145`, `:205`, `:261`). On production today, `storage_unconfigured` is the answer to every cost upload and export (CLAUDE.md, blocked table), so this is live text and not a hypothetical.
4. **Many errors omit what happened to the seller's data or money, and what to do next.** Billing errors are the clearest case: `card_declined` and `billing_unconfigured` do not say that nothing was charged, while the neighbouring `plan_unavailable` and `stripe_error` do.
5. **One failure is worded several ways.**
   - Currency mismatch has five wordings: "this shop sells in" (`cost_uploads.py:323`, `costs.py:98`, `returns.py:369`), "the currency this shop reports in" (`other_sales.py:130`), "the statement's currency" (`settlements.py:346`) and "this shop is read in" (`payouts.py:93`).
   - A period that ends before it starts has four wordings (`costs.py:194`, `returns.py:256`, `exports.py:297`, `settlements.py:353`).
   - A missing cost has five: "product variant has no cost" (`today_view.py:294`), "One variant has no cost price yet." (`products.py:349`), "Some products you sold have no cost" (`tax.py:363`), "Not known: incomplete_costs" (`exports.py:132`) and "This variant had no cost when it sold" (`returns.py:393`).
   - The connection is "TikTok Shop connection" (`tiktok_webhooks.py:157`), "TikTok connection" (`today_view.py:262`) and "TikTok needs reconnecting" (`today_view.py:267`).
   - TikTok being unconfigured has two codes and two texts (`connections.py:104`, `:323`), and the web rewrites only one of them (`ConnectTikTok.jsx:15-16`).
6. **A8 terminology drifts in service output.** The drift covers "Contribution" (insights), "You keep" (CSV export), "On the shelf" (stock notification), "Postage and packing" (insights), "TikTok's cut" (insights), and "TikTok commission", "Creator commission" and "Your payout" (landing page). A8.4 withdraws every one of these. **Conflict between the rulings:** A31.1 names the insight rule `negative_contribution` and quotes "postage and packing" from the QA case. Those names are fine as rule keys, but A8 governs labels a seller reads, and layer 1 wins.
7. **Plan tiers are not enforced, so the feature lists describe differences that do not exist.** No code in `service/app` reads `plan_slug` to gate any feature. `billing.py` is the only reader, and it reads the slug only to create and report the subscription. A Starter seller therefore gets everything Growth advertises. Two features on sale do not exist at all: "Scheduled exports" and "Priority support".
8. **Raw TikTok codes and scope names reach the seller.** See `today_view.py:254`, `today_view.py:257` and `connections.py:275`.
9. **The landing page states no price, plan, trial length or VAT position.** It has no meta description, no privacy notice and no company registration number. Its security and deletion claims go further than the code does.
10. **House style against layer 2 (conflict noted).** Plan feature lists and Needs you labels are noun phrases, which SaaS convention accepts for brevity. The house style requires a subject and a verb in every sentence. Where a string is a label (a list item, a column header), this audit treats it as a label and does not demand a verb. Where it reads as a sentence or a message, the suggested text gives it a full sentence.
