3. What MyShopEdge does

8. Figures and their accuracy

MyShopEdge works out figures that include gross sales, seller discounts, refunds, net sales, TikTok deductions, net proceeds, product costs, gross profit, returns, statements and payouts.

Net proceeds

Net proceeds are what remains after TikTok's deductions and the adjustments TikTok reports, before your product costs.

Gross profit

Gross profit needs the cost of each product sold. Where a cost is missing, MyShopEdge says how much of the period its costs cover, rather than filling the gap with a guess.

Timing

Sales, settlements, statements, refunds, adjustments and payouts happen on different dates. A difference between your sales, your statements and your bank does not by itself mean that something is wrong.
