Cookie Policy

Effective 10 October 2026. Last updated 10 October 2026.

1. What this policy covers

This policy explains how Inspirecraft Global Ltd, trading as MyShopEdge, uses cookies and similar technologies on myshopedge.inspirecraftglobal.com, on the app at app.myshopedge.inspirecraftglobal.com, and on any MyShopEdge page that links here. A cookie is a small text file that a website places on your device.

2. The short version

MyShopEdge sets cookies only to sign you in and keep you signed in. When you open the billing page, Stripe sets its own cookies to take the payment securely and to prevent fraud. MyShopEdge sets no advertising cookie and no tracking cookie, and the website sets no cookie at all. Because every cookie here is strictly necessary for something you asked for, we do not show a cookie banner.

4. Analytics

The app counts page views with Vercel Analytics. It sets no cookie and stores nothing on your device. It does not follow you to other websites.

5. Managing cookies

You can see and delete cookies in your browser's settings, and you can tell your browser to block them. If you block the sign-in cookies, you cannot sign in to MyShopEdge. If you block Stripe's cookies, the card form may not work.

6. Other services

Stripe, and the Google, GitHub or Microsoft page you sign in through, may set their own cookies while you are on their pages. Their own policies explain them.

7. Changes

When the cookies we use change, we update this policy and change the date at the top.
