2. What this policy covers

This policy explains how we collect, use, keep, share and protect personal data when you visit the MyShopEdge website, create or use an account, connect a TikTok Shop, add or upload product costs, create or download exports, choose a plan, or contact us. TikTok Shop and Stripe handle information under their own privacy notices and terms.

3. The personal data we hold

Account data

  • Your name and email address, as your sign-in provider (Google, GitHub or Microsoft) gives them to us when you sign in.
  • An identifier for your sign-in, which lets us recognise you. We never receive or store a password.
  • Your settings, such as your alert choices and whether you want notices by email.
  • Anything you write to us when you ask for help.

Shop data

  • Your shop's name, its identifiers and its region.
  • Products, SKUs, variants and stock levels.
  • Orders and order lines, with their dates, quantities and statuses.
  • Sales, seller discounts, refunds, returns, cancellations, fees, commissions, statements, settlements and payouts.
  • The product costs you enter or upload, and your decisions on returns and stock.
  • Your export settings and the files you ask us to build.

Buyer data

TikTok Shop's records can carry details of your buyers, such as a name, a username, an address, a telephone number or a tracking number. MyShopEdge drops these details before anything is written to its records. We keep the order references and the money, which are what the figures need, and nothing that names or locates a buyer.

Billing data

Stripe takes your card details in a form that Stripe serves on our billing page, and the card number never reaches our systems. We hold the references Stripe gives us: your Stripe customer and subscription identifiers, your plan, your trial and renewal dates, and whether a payment went through.

Technical data

Our hosting providers record the requests that reach them, which include an IP address, the time and the browser's description of itself, so that the service can be run and kept secure. The app counts page views through Vercel Analytics, which sets no cookie (see our Cookie Policy).

Uploaded files

A cost file you upload is read for its product names, SKUs, unit costs and dates. MyShopEdge accepts Excel and CSV files only. Please do not put other personal data in a cost file.

4. Where the data comes from

  • You give it to us when you sign in, enter costs, upload a file, change a setting or write to us.
  • Your sign-in provider gives us your name and email address.
  • TikTok Shop gives us your shop's data after you approve the connection.
  • Stripe tells us about your subscription and its payments.
  • Our hosting providers record technical data as described above.

6. Our lawful bases

Contract. Most of what we do is needed to provide the service you signed up for: your account, your shop's figures, your plan and our replies to you.

Legitimate interests. We keep the service secure, find and fix faults and prevent misuse, because a service that holds financial records has to be safe and working. We weigh these interests against your rights.

Legal obligation. We keep some records because company and tax law requires it, and we answer lawful requests.

7. How we use TikTok Shop data

Once you approve the connection, MyShopEdge reads your shop's orders, returns, products, stock, statements and payouts. It uses them to show and reconcile your sales, fees, refunds and payouts, to work out what the shop has left you, to apply your product costs, to build your exports, to support your return and stock decisions, and to show how close your turnover is to the VAT registration threshold.

MyShopEdge only reads. It does not place orders, issue refunds, change listings or prices, move money or message buyers. We do not sell personal data, and we do not use buyer data for advertising.

8. Automated calculations

MyShopEdge works out figures such as net proceeds, product cost coverage, gross profit, product rankings, the effect of returns, the match between statements and payouts, and progress towards the VAT threshold. These figures are information for you. They are not legal, accounting, tax, financial or investment advice. We make no decision about you by automated means that has a legal or similarly significant effect.

12. Your rights

The law gives you the right to see the personal data we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to receive it in a form you can take elsewhere. You can download your own data under Settings, Your data, and delete your account there too. For anything else, write to privacy@inspirecraftglobal.com. We may need to confirm that a request comes from you. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

13. Email from us

We send email only about your account: notices that need action, and replies to you. You can switch notice emails off under Settings, Profile and plan. We still send what the service cannot run without, such as a reply to a question you asked.

14. Security

Data travels encrypted between your browser, our service and our providers. The database separates each account's records by row level security, so one account cannot read another's. TikTok tokens are stored encrypted. Our staff reach your records only to keep the service running or to answer you. No system is completely secure, so please keep your sign-in account safe and tell us at once if you think someone else has used it.
