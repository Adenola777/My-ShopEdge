Net proceeds are what TikTok Shop has left you before your product costs. They are not your profit.

What the lines mean

Net sales are gross sales less seller discounts. Net proceeds are net sales less TikTok Shop's deductions and refunds. Gross profit is net proceeds less what the products cost you. The seller finance FAQ explains each step, and why a payout can differ from net proceeds for a while: some orders may still be awaiting settlement, a payout can cover more than one statement, and refunds can arrive weeks after the sale.
