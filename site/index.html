The problem

TikTok pays you in pieces.

A sale is not the same as a payout. Platform fees, seller discounts, affiliate commission, shipping, refunds, returns and later adjustments can all change what reaches your bank.

Gross sales£62.00
Seller discounts−£2.00
Platform commission−£5.40
Affiliate commission−£3.10
Shipping fee−£2.75
Partial refund, 3 weeks later−£8.00
Your payout£40.75

Each line is a normal part of selling on TikTok Shop, not a mistake. Together they explain why the payout is smaller than the sale.

MyShopEdge puts every deduction back into one clear picture.

Product costs

Know which products make you money.

Revenue does not tell you what you made. Add your product costs by uploading an Excel or CSV file, or type them in one by one. MyShopEdge matches costs with TikTok Shop sales, fees and returns to show gross profit and margin by product.

Costs are optional. Start with your net proceeds today and add costs whenever you are ready.

Missing costs are clearly shown, so you know when a profit figure is incomplete.

Money

Every fee, refund and adjustment is named and reconciled.

See platform commission, affiliate commission, shipping, promotions, refunds and other deductions separately. Nothing is folded into an unexplained total.

Products

See which products earn you the most, and which cost you.

Rank products by gross profit after returns, see product margin and identify the products that need attention. Where costs are missing, see net proceeds and clear cost-status information instead.

Exports

Your figures, reconciled and ready to export.

Export a monthly summary, a full ledger or one row per order line in Excel or CSV. Choose sales basis to review activity by the day of sale, or cash basis to review it by the month TikTok settled it. A month summary has the same totals as MyShopEdge shows for the same period and basis.

On the Pro plan, schedule weekly or monthly exports and receive an in-app notification when your file is ready.

Returns

Every return is checked, counted once and reflected in your figures.

Mark returns as resellable or unsellable so stock, write-offs and product-profit figures remain accurate.

VAT and tax

Know when you are approaching the VAT threshold.

MyShopEdge tracks available TikTok Shop turnover over a rolling 12-month period against the current £90,000 VAT registration threshold and shows how much headroom remains. For eligible sole traders, it can also provide a planning estimate of Income Tax and National Insurance based on the shop data available. Add product costs for a more useful estimate.

Tax figures are estimates based on the information in MyShopEdge and are not tax advice. Scottish Income Tax rates are not yet included.

Stock

Know what is running out before it does.

See what is on hand, what has sold, what is coming back and what needs attention first.
